f. Fieldwork Dashboard
App information

A clear view of your website business.

Fieldwork Dashboard is a private desktop application used by its owner to review website performance, incoming call activity, and business outcomes across owned websites.

This is the public information page for the app. The dashboard itself runs locally on the operator’s computer. This website does not provide access to the dashboard, account credentials, call records, or business reports.

What Fieldwork does

  • Reads aggregate visitor, session, and phone-button click reports from Google Analytics.
  • Reads search clicks, impressions, and average position from Google Search Console for authorized sites.
  • Reads incoming call history from Twilio for configured website numbers.
  • Stores manually entered inquiry classifications, follow-up actions, costs, and revenue.

How Google access works

The operator authorizes Fieldwork through Google’s consent screen. The app requests read-only Google Analytics and Search Console permissions. It cannot modify Analytics configuration, change website content, or manage Google accounts.

Google report data is used only to display website performance in the local dashboard. Fieldwork does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Read the Fieldwork Privacy Policy for details about data access, storage, retention, and revocation.

Use of the app

Fieldwork is for the operator’s own business review. It is not a public reporting service, and visitors cannot register for access here. Source reports may be delayed or incomplete. A phone-button click or call record does not establish a qualified inquiry or a completed sale; those outcomes require review.
